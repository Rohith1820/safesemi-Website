About SafeSemi

A founder-led supplier, built for one job.

SafeSemi is a newly incorporated, India-based supplier of ESD-safe JEDEC matrix trays, set up specifically to serve the OSAT and ATMP packaging plants ramping up under the India Semiconductor Mission.

We are early-stage by design, not by accident: rather than carrying the overhead of owning a factory before demand is proven, SafeSemi owns the design, material specification, and quality verification for every tray we supply, and produces through vetted, ISO 9001‑aligned contract manufacturing partners. That means lower fixed costs for us, and a faster, more flexible response for you.

We are honest about where we are: currently pre-revenue, working through our first qualification samples. What we can commit to is precision in the one product we've chosen to focus on, full transparency about our manufacturing model, and a direct, single point of contact for every order.

The Problem

India builds the chips. It still imports the tray.

Every OSAT and ATMP facility ramping up under the India Semiconductor Mission needs a steady supply of ESD-safe handling trays — and right now, the large majority are sourced from Taiwan, China, Korea, or the US.

The import cycle

Six-week lead times, customs duty, and fixed minimum order quantities that don't flex with a ramping production line.

The trust gap

Trays are typically accepted on a spec sheet alone. One documented failure: a tray rated ESD-safe tested at 6.2×10¹&sup9; Ω — nine orders of magnitude out of spec — and a 561V discharge followed.

Frequently Asked

What clients usually ask us first.

Do you own your own manufacturing facility?

Not yet. We design and quality-verify every product in-house, and produce through established, ISO 9001‑aligned contract manufacturers. This keeps our costs — and your pricing — free of idle factory overhead while we prove out demand. We're transparent about this from the first conversation.

How do you verify ESD compliance?

Every qualification batch is sent to an independent third-party lab for surface resistivity testing, following ESDA STM11.11 methodology. You receive the test report directly, not just a supplier spec sheet.

What's your minimum order quantity?

500 units for a first qualification/trial batch. We size the order to your actual need, not to a factory's minimum run.

Can you match a custom pocket grid for our specific package?

Yes. The outer frame is fixed to the JEDEC standard, but the pocket grid is designed around your exact chip dimensions once you share them with us.

Are you ISO 9001 certified?

Certification is in progress. Our contract manufacturing partners are already ISO 9001‑certified, and we are completing our own certification alongside our first qualification runs.
